Primary references

  • Turing (1950), Computing Machinery and Intelligence

    The imitation-game measurement framing; university-hosted primary paper.

  • McCarthy et al. (1955), Dartmouth proposal

    1955 proposal for 1956 workshop; language, abstraction, and intelligence aims.

  • McCulloch and Pitts (1943), A Logical Calculus

    Connections between neuron-like networks and propositional logic.

  • Rumelhart, Hinton and Williams (1986), Learning representations by back-propagating errors

    Backpropagation through layered networks; author-hosted primary paper.

  • On the Opportunities and Risks of Foundation Models

    Broad pretraining and adaptation across downstream tasks.

  • BERT

    Masked-language-model pretraining differs from next-token prediction.

  • Attention Is All You Need

    Transformer architecture, dot-product attention, and masking forbidden pre-softmax positions.

  • GPT-4 Technical Report

    Explicit limits on architecture and training disclosure; no speculative model-size claims imported.

  • NumPy: Copies and views

    Shared storage, basic versus advanced indexing, reshape copy/view behavior.

  • NumPy: matmul

    Final-two-axis matrix multiplication and broadcast leading axes; scalars rejected.

  • NumPy: Broadcasting

    Trailing-axis compatibility and singleton expansion; copies need not be materialized.

  • einops: einsum

    Named-axis contractions with unmentioned output axes summed.

  • einops: rearrange

    Axis permutations and split/merge patterns.

  • einops: reduce

    Named-axis reduction; absent output axes aggregated with specified operation.
